## 📸 Image Guidelines

### Logo
- **Format**: PNG with transparent background
- **Size**: 200x200px minimum
- **Content**: Your brand mark or restaurant initial
- **Colors**: High contrast for visibility

### Cover Photo
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: 1200x600px (or 2:1 ratio)
- **Content**: Interior ambiance, signature dish, or outdoor seating
- **Quality**: High resolution, well-lit

### Menu Item Images
- **Format**: JPG
- **Size**: 400x400px minimum
- **Content**: The actual dish, plated professionally
- **Tip**: Photograph from a 45° angle for best results
