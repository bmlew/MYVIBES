## 🔍 DOMAIN AVAILABILITY CHECKLIST

Before finalizing, check:
- [ ] .co.za domain available
- [ ] .com domain available (for future expansion)
- [ ] .app domain available (for app stores)
- [ ] Social media handles (@name on Instagram, Twitter, Facebook)
- [ ] Trademark search in South Africa
- [ ] Google search to check existing businesses

### How to Check:
1. **Domains:** Go to domains.co.za or namecheap.com
2. **Trademarks:** cipc.co.za (SA Companies Registry)
3. **Social:** Check Instagram.com/username, Twitter.com/username
4. **Google:** Search "[name] restaurant" or "[name] app"

## 💡 NAMING BEST PRACTICES

### A Great Name Should:
✅ Be easy to spell and pronounce
✅ Be memorable (pass the "radio test")
✅ Convey what you do (or be easily explained)
✅ Work in multiple languages (SA has 11!)
✅ Be short (2-3 syllables ideal)
✅ Have available domain (.co.za minimum)
✅ Avoid negative connotations in any language
✅ Be future-proof (not too trendy)

### Avoid:
❌ Hard-to-spell made-up words
❌ Numbers or hyphens
❌ Names too similar to competitors
❌ Names that limit future expansion
❌ Acronyms (unless super catchy)
