# 🎯 Admin Guide: Configurable Rewards

## Quick Start

**Location:** Admin Portal → Platform Settings & Configuration → Partner & Influencer Rewards

---

## Settings Overview

### 1. Customer Referral Rewards

| Setting | What It Does | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Download Bounty** | Partner earns this when customer downloads app | R20 instant payment |
| **Check-in Threshold** | How many check-ins needed for reward | Every 100 check-ins |
| **Threshold Reward** | Amount paid when threshold reached | R200 per milestone |

**Impact:**
- Lower threshold = More frequent rewards = Higher partner engagement
- Higher bounty = More aggressive customer acquisition

---

### 2. Business Subscription Commissions

| Setting | What It Does | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Commission %** | Percentage of subscription payment | 15% of R499 = R75 |
| **Recurring** | Pay every month vs one-time only | ✅ = Monthly income |

**Impact:**
- Higher % = More business referrals but higher costs
- Recurring enabled = Partner earns EVERY month business pays

---

### 3. Partner Engagement Bonuses

| Setting | What It Does | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Partner Visit Bonus** | Extra points for visiting their referred business | +50 points |
| **Regular Check-in** | Points for any check-in | 10 points |

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Boost Customer Downloads

**Goal:** Get more app downloads

**Action:**
```
Download Bounty: R20 → R40 ⬆️ (double it!)
```

**Result:** Partners promote app more aggressively

---

### Scenario 2: Increase Customer Activity

**Goal:** Get customers to visit businesses more

**Action:**
```
Check-in Threshold: 100 → 50 ⬇️ (easier to reach)
Threshold Reward: R200 → R300 ⬆️ (bigger reward)
```

**Result:** Partners encourage customers to check in

---

### Scenario 3: Attract More Businesses

**Goal:** Get businesses to join platform

**Action:**
```
Commission %: 15% → 25% ⬆️
Recurring: ✅ Enable
```

**Result:** Partners focus on business referrals  
**Note:** This increases monthly costs!

---

### Scenario 4: Reduce Costs

**Goal:** Lower partner payouts

**Action:**
```
Commission %: 15% → 10% ⬇️
Recurring: ❌ Disable (one-time only)
Check-in Threshold: 100 → 200 ⬆️
```

**Result:** Lower expenses, less partner motivation

---

## How Recurring Commissions Work

### Example: Business Subscription

**Settings:**
- Subscription: R499/month
- Commission: 15%
- Recurring: ✅ Enabled

**Partner Earnings:**
```
Month 1: R499 × 15% = R75 ✅
Month 2: R499 × 15% = R75 ✅
Month 3: R499 × 15% = R75 ✅
...
Year 1 Total: R75 × 12 = R900
```

**If Recurring Disabled:**
```
Month 1: R499 × 15% = R75 ✅
Month 2: R0 ❌
Month 3: R0 ❌
Total: R75 (one-time)
```

## Cost Calculator

### Monthly Partner Costs

**Assumptions:**
- 100 active partners
- Each has 5 customers (avg 10 check-ins/month)
- Each has 2 referred businesses

**Customer Costs:**
```
Downloads: 100 partners × 5 customers × R20 = R10,000 (one-time)
Check-ins: Minimal (threshold not reached monthly)
```

**Business Costs (Recurring Enabled):**
```
Businesses: 100 partners × 2 businesses = 200 businesses
Subscriptions: 200 × R499 = R99,800/month revenue
Commissions: R99,800 × 15% = R14,970/month
```

**Total Monthly Cost:** ~R15,000

**If Commission Increased to 25%:**
```
Commissions: R99,800 × 25% = R24,950/month
Increase: +R9,980/month
```

## Best Practices

### 1. Start Conservative
- Begin with default values
- Monitor partner performance
- Adjust gradually

### 2. Track Metrics
- Partner acquisition rate
- Customer download rate
- Business signup rate
- Total monthly costs

### 3. Seasonal Adjustments
- Boost rewards during slow months
- Reduce during high activity
- Run limited-time promotions

### 4. Test Changes
- Change one setting at a time
- Monitor impact for 2-4 weeks
- Revert if needed

## Emergency Controls

### Stop All New Commissions
```
Download Bounty: → R0
Threshold Reward: → R0
Commission %: → 0%
```
**Note:** Existing commitments still honored

### Pause Partner Program
- Set all rewards to R0
- Disable recurring commissions
- Communicate with partners first!

---

## Partner Communication

When changing settings, consider announcing:

**Good Changes (Increases):**
```
🎉 Great news! We're increasing rewards:
- Download bounty: R20 → R30
- Commission: 15% → 20%

Your hard work deserves more! 💰
```

**Bad Changes (Decreases):**
```
⚠️ Important Update:
To ensure sustainability, we're adjusting:
- Commission: 15% → 12%

Existing commissions not affected.
Thank you for your understanding.
```
