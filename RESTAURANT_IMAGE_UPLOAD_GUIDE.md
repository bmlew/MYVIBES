# 📸 Restaurant Image Upload Guide

## Where to Upload Restaurant Images

### Quick Navigation
1. **Open Business Dashboard** → Click on **"Settings"** in the left sidebar
2. You'll see **TWO** upload sections at the top of Settings:

---

## 1️⃣ Business Logo Upload
**Location:** Settings → First section (orange/purple gradient card)

**What it's for:**
- Your restaurant's logo (square/circular icon)
- Shows in sidebar navigation
- Displays in customer app
- Appears across the platform

**Specifications:**
- **Format:** PNG, JPG, GIF, or WebP
- **Size:** Maximum 2MB
- **Recommended dimensions:** Square (1:1 ratio), minimum 200x200px
- **Example:** Your restaurant's circular logo icon

**How to upload:**
1. Click "Choose File" button
2. Select your logo image
3. Preview appears instantly
4. Click the **"Upload"** button (orange/purple gradient)
5. Done! ✓

---

## 2️⃣ Restaurant Cover Image Upload (NEW!)
**Location:** Settings → Second section (purple/orange gradient card)

**What it's for:**
- Large banner/hero image for your restaurant
- Showcases your restaurant's ambiance
- Displays signature dishes
- Shows interior/exterior photos
- Featured on your business profile

**Specifications:**
- **Format:** PNG, JPG, GIF, or WebP
- **Size:** Maximum 5MB
- **Recommended dimensions:** Landscape (16:9 ratio), minimum 1200x675px
- **Example:** Beautiful photo of your restaurant interior, popular dish, or ambiance

**How to upload:**
1. Click "Choose File" button
2. Select your cover image
3. Large preview (64px height) appears instantly
4. Click the **"Upload"** button (purple/orange gradient)
5. Done! ✓

---

## Smart Reminders

### Overview Dashboard Banner
- If you haven't uploaded a **logo**, you'll see a prominent banner at the top of your Overview
- If you haven't uploaded a **cover image**, the banner will remind you
- Click "Upload Images Now" → Takes you directly to Settings

### Visual Indicators
- ✓ **Logo uploaded** = Shows in sidebar with your business name
- ✓ **Cover uploaded** = Shows large preview in Settings
- ✗ **Not uploaded** = Dotted border placeholder with icon

---

## Quick Tips
- Upload BOTH for best customer impression
- High-quality images build trust
- Update anytime by uploading new versions
- Images are cached for offline viewing

---

## Need Help?
