# MYVIBES Investor Deck

## Executive Summary

**MYVIBES** is a mobile-first platform revolutionizing how South African customers discover and engage with restaurants and hotels. Through our freemium model, AI-powered recommendations, and innovative affiliate program, we're building the premier hospitality discovery platform in South Africa.

### Key Highlights
- 🎯 **Target Market**: South African restaurants, hotels, and hospitality venues
- 📱 **Platform**: Progressive Web App (PWA) - iOS, Android, and Web compatible
- 💰 **Revenue Model**: Hybrid (B2B Subscriptions + Affiliate Commissions)
- 🚀 **Current Traction**: 10K+ Active Users | 500+ Partner Venues | 50K+ Reviews | 4.8★ Rating

---

## Problem Statement

### Market Challenges
1. **Discovery Gap**: Customers struggle to find quality dining experiences that match their preferences
2. **Limited Visibility**: Small to medium restaurants lack affordable marketing channels
3. **Fragmented Experience**: Existing platforms focus on delivery, not discovery and engagement
4. **Trust Deficit**: Limited authentic reviews and real-time information
5. **High Commission Costs**: Traditional platforms charge 15-30% per transaction

## Our Solution

MYVIBES connects customers with the perfect dining experiences through:

### For Customers
- **AI-Powered Recommendations**: Personalized suggestions based on preferences, location, and behavior
- **Real-Time Discovery**: Live specials, exclusive events, and venue availability
- **Social Reviews**: Authentic rating and review system with photo sharing
- **Age-Appropriate Venues**: Filter establishments by suitable age groups (Family, 18+, 21+, etc.)
- **Seamless Reservations**: Book tables directly through the platform
- **PWA Experience**: Native app experience without app store downloads

### For Businesses
- **Affordable Marketing**: Flat monthly fee (R499-R999) vs. per-transaction fees
- **Comprehensive Dashboard**: Analytics, customer insights, and performance metrics
- **Direct Customer Connection**: Build relationships without intermediaries
- **Reservation Management**: Automated booking system with confirmations
- **Growth Tools**: Affiliate network driving new business discovery
- **Brand Control**: Showcase menu, photos, specials, and events
